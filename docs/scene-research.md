# Scene Research And Art Direction

This is a fan-made reconstruction, not an official floor plan or licensed
asset set. Real-world details are constraints for plausibility; room layouts
stay readable point-and-click spaces first.

## Source-Grounded Details

- Public production reporting describes Rocket Beans TV as a Hamburg production
  campus in and around Heinrichstrasse with three buildings.
- Reports place the day start in Heinrichstrasse 9. Shortly behind the entrance
  on the right: Dispo; beside it: Techniklager with cameras, cables and
  microphones; one stair farther up: Morning Call in a large long office.
- House 11 is reported as containing offices and studios.
- House 15 is described as a former bicycle workshop that houses scenography /
  set design plus more studios and offices.
- Public reporting describes rack-based server infrastructure, ATEM-style
  switchers, a Universal Videohub/crossbar, self-laid SDI routes, and an
  80-meter SDI route from the server room in number 11 to the studio in number
  9.
- Production reporting describes several studios, low ceiling height as a
  lighting constraint, Sony PXW-X160 studio cameras, and regie workflows with
  image mixer, audio and graphics/interactivity roles.
- A public `Verflixxte Klixx` episode listing references Greenscreen-Helden.
  The game treats a green-screen studio as a known public cue, not as an exact
  studio plan.
- The Hamburg city scenes use public landmark identity rather than exact
  production reporting: Schanzenstrasse/Sternschanze as nearby urban texture,
  Bahnhof Sternschanze as transit timing, Schanzenpark with the water tower and
  distant TV-tower view as lighting reference, and Karoviertel as a plausible
  print-shop/material errand.

## Scene Decisions

- `greenscreen_studio`: large green-screen wall, floor marks, compact Klixx
  table, chat/test monitor, studio camera, regie exit and a headset production
  silhouette. The lower floor is intentionally clear for the animated intern.
- `office_hall`: follows the Heinrichstrasse 9 entrance sequence: courtyard
  door, Dispo board, Techniklager, stairs, studio/regie/server-room routes and
  city exit.
- `building_courtyard`: three-building yard with office entrance, Haus 15
  workshop route, loading zone, address cue and city gate.
- `server_room`: rack towers, video hub/crossbar, label printer and SDI spool
  for the long signal route.
- `set_workshop`: former-workshop traces, scenic flats, gaffer tape, tool wall
  and extra studio door.
- `control_room`: spacious regie with monitor wall, preview of studio/city
  layer, graphics terminal, intercom and on-air lamp.
- `schanzenstrasse`: first city expansion; street mural, kiosk, production
  notice and exits that make the map suddenly larger.
- `sternschanze_station`: railway/station edge with clock, platform sign shape,
  busker and route toward Schanzenpark.
- `schanzenpark`: water tower, park paths, city reflector and distant TV-tower
  view used as a light reference for the studio.
- `karoviertel`: copyshop, record/poster store and market boxes; this supplies
  the lower-third card and hints that the city could keep expanding.

## Interaction And Style

- The visual target is a serious mid-1990s cinematic VGA point-and-click look:
  pulled-back rooms, readable silhouettes, moody lighting, visible walking
  floor and embedded props. Generated source images are lightly posterized and
  scaled with nearest-neighbor filtering to avoid a photorealistic read.
- The interaction model is explicit verb-object adventure play: visible verbs
  for looking, poking, using, picking up, tasting, smelling and talking, plus
  inventory selection for item-on-object puzzles. Exits stay forgiving and work
  regardless of the selected verb.
- The story is now about `Fall 000`, a missing-host archive case. The
  initially practical studio tasks become time-anchor work: campus signal
  route, city clock, park light, Copyshop materials and a begehbarer
  Schimmelbrüder video frame all feed the final rescue setup.
